# ADR-0015 — Owner-requested persistent conversations and visual locale

Accepted for implementation, 2026-10-02. The owner explicitly chose history across restarts, overriding the attached plan's ephemeral-only assumption. Use host-authored schema-1 Meta-only transcripts, separately encrypted current-user private storage, 128 conversations/512 KiB each/20 continuation turns and bounded domain records. Never silently evict/truncate. Native-confirmed removal hides the conversation using a durable tombstone; encrypted recovery records and used quota remain. This is not permanent/secure erasure or compaction. Finances/secrets/file-recovery are independent. Persist accepted intent and final display status; restored incomplete turns are interrupted and never restore requests, grants, approvals or continuation authority. Re-admit saved complete content before transmission. No renderer history uploads/localStorage or old provider import. The owner's supplied assets and British-English requirements supersede the plan's central version title/Spanish copy. Accessible borderless local menus, stationary branding/settings, scrolling selected history, bounded composer and named Send/Stop are required; no unimplemented icon grants capability.
