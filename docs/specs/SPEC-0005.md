No claim is made that a visual page preview reveals every hidden PDF object or establishes Contributor eligibility; the native review requires owner confirmation of the complete source, including hidden content/metadata.

Product bounds are independent of ChatGPT and stricter than the provider: four current attachments; immutable sources at most 8 MiB each and 16 MiB per current batch; 20 PDF pages total in a batch, all rendered at 150 DPI for processed display; image dimensions at most 4,096 each and 4,194,304 decoded pixels; one frame for GIF; at most 4 MiB of normalised PNG output per file, 16 MiB of processed PNG per batch and 64 MiB decoded RGBA per batch. Refuse rather than omit, crop, resize silently, select only some pages or truncate. Text/data files are strict bounded UTF-8, inert (no XML/YAML/JSON/formula execution); the composed prompt including text attachment context must still meet the inherited 32-KiB user-message bound. The 128-KiB individual/256-KiB aggregate parser text ceilings are hard defensive bounds, not promises that every such file fits the smaller sending bound. A larger text document can be converted externally to PDF by the owner.

Complex parsing runs in a fixed byte-only child worker assigned to an active-process-one, kill-on-close Job before parser execution, with 256-MiB memory, 10-second CPU and 15-second wall limits. A low-integrity/restricted token and absent secret environment reduce authority but are not independently verified no-network/RCE confinement; no network/file-path/converter authority is exposed by the worker protocol. Qualification must cover actual termination, fail-closed launch errors and native PDF/image processing, not just a thread timeout.

After Job termination/closure, one non-blocking worker-admission guard and a separate 1,000-ms/200-round cleanup attempt repeatedly cancel live owned I/O and join only completed, signalled threads. Incomplete cleanup denies and poisons further admission until process restart; at most three unfinished I/O closures may retain handles/bounded buffers until actual completion/process exit. This does not guarantee completed cleanup, physical secure erasure or OS scheduling/response. [ADR-0019](../adr/ADR-0019.md) fixes these source controls; complete actual-executable worker/resource/teardown qualification remains pending, distinct from the controlled held-pipe fixture's scoped P3 coverage above.

Protocol/policy advances to 4: authenticated native selection returns only bounded opaque attachment metadata. Source advertising a capability does not qualify it for final engineering delivery; shipping qualification must exercise its complete route. An additional mixed-egress ceiling admits at most 16 MiB combined native PDF-original and normalised-image bytes before base64 encoding, independently of the source and processed-PNG batch ceilings. The exact immutable serialised body must then fit 24 MiB. Neither ceiling increases model context/completion limits, changes the fixed destination/rates or assumes bytes equal tokens; refuse excess without truncation. Images are normalised PNG without copied metadata; source originals remain temporary. The existing full-context reservation remains mandatory and new usage validation must still refuse ambiguous/unbounded usage. Original PDF/image bytes never become persistent processed-media records.

“Lossless PNG” means lossless encoding of the normalised decoded RGBA8 pixels, not bit-perfect retention of original bytes, precision or metadata. Sixteen-bit image samples may be quantised to eight bits and JPEG colour-space conversion can change representation. Decoded EXIF orientation is applied before PNG generation; its metadata is discarded. The owner must inspect the processed preview before approval. Persistent processed content is not an original-fidelity archive; native PDF egress remains the exact original bytes.

ADR-0020 fixes the independent encrypted processed-media-v1 domain: 6-MiB plaintext record, 128-MiB aggregate domain and 512-record ceilings; 16-MiB processed content per conversation and 4-MiB per attachment. Existing text-history/finance domain names and bytes are not rewritten. Partial appends can leave unexposed processed-only orphans and poison writes until checked replay; no silent reset, deletion or quota reclamation occurs. A new selection replaces all old temporary originals, including on cancellation, while preserving the draft. Grants expire after 300 seconds and are checked immediately before dispatch after native review. Native inspection admits the complete bounded visual batch: up to 20 PDF pages plus image pages within four files (24 total maximum), with the unchanged aggregate decoded-memory bound.
